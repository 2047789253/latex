\abstract{With the large-scale development of intelligent warehousing, efficient path planning for Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) in dynamic Robotic Mobile Fulfillment Systems (RMFS) has become a critical challenge. Traditional heuristic algorithms struggle to adapt to dynamic environments, while standard Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) often faces issues of sparse rewards and convergence difficulties under complex constraints. To this end, this paper proposes a single-AGV path planning algorithm that fuses heuristic guidance with DRL, termed DRS-DQN (D* Lite Reward Shaping-DQN). This method innovatively utilizes the dynamic distance field maintained by the D* Lite algorithm as a potential function to construct a back-tracking reward shaping mechanism, providing dense reward gradients while ensuring the invariance of the optimal policy. To address the issue of outdated experience in dynamic environments, a delayed calculation mechanism and Prioritized Experience Replay (PER) are introduced. Experiments conducted in five RMFS simulation environments of varying scales (54 to 156 storage stations) and layouts (rectangular and fishbone) demonstrate that DRS-DQN stably converges and completes tasks with a 100\% success rate. In contrast, standard DQN fails to converge in the same scenarios. Furthermore, the experiments confirm that the fishbone layout significantly outperforms the traditional rectangular layout in terms of reinforcement learning training efficiency.}

\keywords{Automated Guided Vehicle, Path Planning, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Reward Shaping, Dynamic Environment, Robotic Mobile Fulfillment System (RMFS)}
